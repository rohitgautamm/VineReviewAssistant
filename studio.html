🛡️ Amazon Vine Community Guidelines Protection Rules

🚫 1. No Medical or Cure Claims

Never state a product cures, heals, treats, or prevents medical conditions (pain, insomnia, eczema, anxiety, arthritis, inflammation). Use subjective comfort phrasing instead.

📦 2. No Shipping, Seller or Delivery Complaints

Do not mention delivery speed, Prime drivers, outer Amazon shipping boxes/bubble mailers, or seller customer service. Review only the product itself.

🏷️ 3. No Vine / Free Product / ETV Mentions

Amazon automatically adds the "Vine Customer Review of Free Product" badge. Mentioning "got this free" or "ETV" inside the text can trigger moderation filters.

🏪 4. No Competing Stores, Links or Counterfeit Claims

Avoid naming Walmart, Temu, AliExpress, Target, or eBay, including URLs, or calling an item "counterfeit/scam". Describe physical materials objectively.
